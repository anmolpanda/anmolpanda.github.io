I am a Ph.D. Candidate (ABD) in Information Science at the University of Michigan, Ann Arbor, specializing in digital media, political behavior, and computational social science. I expect to complete my dissertation in July 2026.

Dissertation

Digital Media, Race, and Political Behavior: Examining Asian American Attitudes and Voting Behavior in the Trump Era

My dissertation uses nationally representative survey data and original survey instruments to study how social media and news media shape racialized attitudes (e.g., racial resentment, views on immigration and policing) and voting behavior among Asian American communities. Methodologically, it combines causal inference, computational text analysis, and public opinion research to understand how digital information environments differentially affect minoritized groups.
