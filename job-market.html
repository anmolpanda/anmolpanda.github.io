Job Market

I am a Postdoctoral Researcher in the Management Information Systems Department at Purdue University's Daniels School of Business, working with Yu (Jeffrey) Hu. I received my Ph.D. in Information from the University of Michigan in 2026. I am on the 2026–27 academic job market for tenure-track positions.

Research Areas

  • Computational social science and political communication
  • Economics of information systems: digital platforms, news business models, and platform governance
  • Trust in AI systems that organizations deploy, and generative AI for data curation
  • Social media, race, and political behavior

Methods

Large-scale NLP (transformer models and LLMs), causal inference (staggered difference-in-differences, synthetic controls), and survey and experimental designs.

Dissertation

How Media, Engagement, and Identities Relate to Political Behavior among Asian Americans

Using Cooperative Election Study data and an original survey, my dissertation studies how social media use and news media diets relate to racialized attitudes (such as views on immigration, policing, and affirmative action) and to political support, and how these relationships differ across racial groups.

Committee: Libby Hemphill (Chair), Joyojeet Pal, Matthew Bui, and Ted Brader, University of Michigan.
